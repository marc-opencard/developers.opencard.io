```
Account (you)
├── OAuth Clients
├── Users (your team)
├── Billings ← invoice recipient; groups orgs on YOUR OpenCard invoice
│   ├── name_legal, organization_number
│   ├── email_invoice, your_reference_invoice
│   └── Organizations (many orgs → one billing_id)
├── Card Issuers (enabled list)
├── TPAs
│   ├── Signatories (email → eID sign)
│   ├── Legal Text (snapshot)
│   └── Signed Document (PDF)
└── Organizations
    ├── reference_id ← YOUR ID
    ├── billing_id → which invoice group this org belongs to
    ├── Webhooks
    │   ├── Headers (custom)
    │   ├── Groups (buyer-party filter)
    │   └── Events (delivery log)
    └── Card Holders
        ├── reference_id ← YOUR ID
        ├── PDPC (email → eID consent)
        └── Cards (from issuer)
            └── Transactions
                ├── TransactionStates
                ├── Receipts
                ├── TrueVAT
                ├── LineItems
                └── EnvironmentalImpact
```

## Entity details

### Account
Your partner account. Created at registration. Contains billing profiles, OAuth clients, enabled issuers.

### Billing
How OpenCard invoices your EMS. Legal entity + invoice email. Multiple organizations can share one `billing_id` so their usage rolls up on a single invoice from OpenCard.

**Key fields:** `id`, `name_legal`, `organization_number`, `email_invoice`, `your_reference_invoice`

→ [Billing guide](/ems/billing)

### Organization
One of your clients. Links to a TPA and a billing profile. Has its own webhook config.

**Key fields:** `id`, `reference_id`, `tpa_id`, `billing_id`, `account_id`, `name`

### Card Holder
A person. Links to organization. Has PDPC consent.

**Key fields:** `id`, `reference_id`, `organization_id`, `identity_id`, `meta.signed`, `meta.pdpc_url`, `identity.name`, `identity.employee_id` (on list)

### TPA
Legal agreement per client company + card issuer combo.

**Statuses:** `pending-signatures` → `pending-activation` → `activated`

### Webhook
Per-organization event subscription. Must pass challenge before `active=true`.

**Key fields:** `url`, `secret`, `active`, `enabled`, event boolean flags, `authentication_type`

### Transaction
One purchase. Same `id` across all states.

**Comes via webhook**, not primarily via API query.
