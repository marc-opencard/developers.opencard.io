title: Card issuer
description: Card issuer entity in the OpenCard EMS data model — bank or card program you enable on your account so it pushes card transactions to OpenCard via TPAs.
---

Bank or card program. You enable issuers on your account, then create TPAs against them. Card issuers manage cards and push transaction data to OpenCard.

Not the same audience as EMS — card issuers use `receipts.opencard.io` for digital receipt matching.
