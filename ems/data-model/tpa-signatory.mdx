---
title: TPA signatory
description: A TPA signatory is an authorized representative who signs the Transaction Processing Authorization for the client company via an emailed eID signing link.
---

Authorized person who signs the TPA on behalf of the client company. Receives email with signing link; signs with eID.

**Key fields:** `id`, `email`, `name`, `signed`, `signed_at`
