---
title: Migrating from Direct Issuer
description: Switch from legacy issuer feed to OpenCard without duplicate transactions.
---

When you onboard a client to OpenCard, they might already have a direct card issuer integration feeding transactions into your EMS.

---

## The problem

During transition you could get **duplicate transactions** — same purchase from both old feed and OpenCard.

---

## Recommended approach

1. **Per card holder**, switch when you receive `card_holder.identified`
2. At that point, **stop the old issuer feed** for that person
3. OpenCard delivers all transactions since last credit card invoice
4. Dedupe on `card.transaction.cleared` using `card_issuer_reference`:

```javascript
if (await db.hasTransactionByIssuerRef(payload.card_issuer_reference)) {
  // Already imported from old feed — skip
  return;
}
```

---

## Timeline

```
Day 0:  TPA signed, org created, card holders added
Day 1:  Cardholders sign PDPC
Day 2:  card_holder.identified → STOP old feed for that person
Day 2+: Transactions flow via OpenCard only
```

Communicate the switch date to your client. No transactions should fall through the gap.
